# d2-rs-fontface source mapping

Native port of `d2renderers/internal/fontface` at D2 master `6a7b48378`
(`/Users/nelson/code/d2`), with the parts of its dependencies whose
behavior is observable through the package: `golang.org/x/image v0.45.0`
`font/sfnt` (outline reader), `golang.org/x/text v0.41.0` `unicode/bidi`, and
`go-text/typesetting v0.3.4` (segmenter, `Segmenter.Split`, `HarfbuzzShaper`,
COLR/CPAL tables). Shaping itself runs on the workspace's vendored
`rustybuzz-go-v090`. Go names map to snake_case; Go `(value, ok, err)` triples
become `Result<Option<_>, Error>` where `Err` means "matched but failed".

## ARM64 FMA sites

`.reference/isometric-port/fma-sites-foundations.txt` lists three fontface
sites, all fusing an exact power-of-two product, so fusion cannot change a
result; they are still written with `go_float`:

- `colrv1.go:306` `(*colrv1Builder).paint` FMSUBDx2: `1 - sx` fused with the
  inlined `fixed214` product `float64(ScaleX) * 2^-14` (`go_fmsub`).
- `shaping.go:321` and `:329` `ShapeTextTransient` FMADDDx1:
  `runPen + XOffset * 2^-6` and `runPen += XAdvance * 2^-6` (`go_fmadd`).

## Not ported

- `postscript.go` (CFF/Type 2 charstrings) and dfont resources: `OTTO` and
  dfont sources fail with explicit `sfnt: ... not supported by the d2-rs
  translation` errors. All bundled D2 fonts and Noto Color Emoji are glyf.
- go-text `Face.Describe` (used only by d2fonts' system fallback ranking) is
  not exposed; `ShapingFont::is_monospace` ports `IsMonospace`.
- Shaping limitations inherited from rustybuzz: go-text scales each GPOS
  value record separately before summing; the rebuilt positions truncate the
  summed delta. No scoreboard record exercises a difference.
- Error texts that come from go-text's font parser differ (the parse is
  rustybuzz/ttf-parser); only malformed fonts reach them.

## Evidence

`scripts/fontface-scoreboard.py` (Go side `tools/fontface-probe`, built in the
trace checkout as `d2renderers/cmd/d2rs-fontface-probe` because Go only
admits importers of `internal/` under `d2renderers/`; binary
`.reference/bin/d2-fontface-probe-6a7b4837`; Rust side `examples/fontface-probe.rs`)
compares records value by value (floats by bits, errors as text):

- labels of every upstream e2e golden at 64 ppem and their font size:
  4,642/4,642 shaping records and 426/426 LoadGlyph outlines match;
- `--extended`: 64,019/64,019 records, adding 2,160 synthetic stress shapings
  (marks, bidi, brackets, embeddings/isolates, complex scripts, emoji
  sequences, ignorables, controls, placeholders), every glyph of the ten D2
  fonts and Noto Color Emoji (LoadGlyph, GlyphAdvance, GlyphRenderBounds at
  two sizes, COLR0GlyphLayers, COLRv1 plan results for all 41,863 emoji glyphs) and font-level
  Metrics/Bounds/cmap/SupportsRenderableRune/Kern.

`cargo test --profile diagnostic -p d2-rs-fontface` covers the COLRv1 plans
against the pinned v0.9.0 fixture, registry authentication, shaping limit and
cancellation errors, placeholders, fallback affinity, sfnt contours, TTC/CFF
handling, default-ignorables, and Go-observed grapheme and bidi runs.
